Terms of Use

Pinewood Tech Club · Schoology Recap

Schoology Recap is a project built by students for students. We treat your data with care and respect.

We are not affiliated with Schoology or PowerSchool in any way. This is an unofficial tool. Use at your own risk.

What we do with your data

  • Process your assignment submission times to generate your recap
  • Email you when your recap is ready
  • Create anonymous aggregate statistics about student behavior
  • Record anonymous telemetry to improve the product

What we don't do

  • Store any Schoology data beyond the statistics shown in your recap
  • Sell or share your data with any third parties beyond those listed below
  • Access content — we only look at submission times, course names, and grades

Hosting

This service runs on a computer owned by Pinewood School, operated on Pinewood School networks. Your data does not leave Pinewood infrastructure except as described below.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare — DNS and network tunneling. Cloudflare may receive your IP address and encrypted traffic metadata in transit. Privacy Policy
  • Schoology — assignment data via their API. Privacy Policy

Questions? Contact us at techclub@pinewood.edu.
